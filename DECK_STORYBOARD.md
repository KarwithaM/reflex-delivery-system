# Reflex: Executive Deck Storyboard

## Slide 1: The Problem
**Takeaway:** Kenyan retailers are losing money and customers because delivery tracking is a messy, invisible WhatsApp group chat.
**Visual:** A screenshot of a chaotic, unreadable WhatsApp group with 500 messages.

## Slide 2: The Solution (Reflex)
**Takeaway:** Reflex brings order to the chaos by giving retailers visibility, dispatchers control, and riders a simple way to update status.
**Visual:** A clean 3-step diagram: Retailer sends order -> Dispatcher assigns rider -> Rider updates status.

## Slide 3: The Architecture
**Takeaway:** We meet the users where they are: WhatsApp for retailers, Web for dispatchers, and SMS for riders.
**Visual:** A simple system diagram showing the 3 user types connecting to our central Node.js backend and Database.

## Slide 4: The Trade-offs
**Takeaway:** We chose high user adoption over low infrastructure costs, and accepted manual dispatching to launch quickly.
**Visual:** A simple table showing the 3 trade-offs from our Trade-off Log.

## Slide 5: The Roadmap
**Takeaway:** Phase 1 is manual dispatch and WhatsApp. Phase 2 is automated routing and offline mobile apps.
**Visual:** A simple timeline graphic showing "Now", "Next", and "Later".
