# Reflex: Demo Script

**Slide 1 (Problem):** "Imagine a busy pharmacy in Nairobi. The phone is ringing, and a WhatsApp group has 500 unread messages about deliveries. This is the chaos we are solving."

**Slide 2 (Solution):** "Reflex brings order. The retailer sends a WhatsApp message. The dispatcher sees it on a clean web dashboard. The rider gets an SMS."

**Slide 3 (Architecture):** "We meet the users where they are. WhatsApp for the retailer, Web for the dispatcher, and SMS for the rider. [Point to diagram] All data flows through our Node.js backend."

**Slide 4 (Trade-offs):** "We chose high adoption over low cost. WhatsApp costs money, but it guarantees the retailer will actually use the system. We also accepted manual dispatching to launch quickly."

**Slide 5 (Roadmap):** "Phase 1 is live. Phase 2 introduces automated routing and offline apps for rural riders."
